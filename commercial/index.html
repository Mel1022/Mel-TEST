Whatever's wrong with it — we fix it

Parts on every truck — closers, hinges, latches, seals, panic devices, fire-rated hardware.
All major hardware brands — LCN · Norton · Dorma · Von Duprin · Detex · Yale · Sargent · Schlage.

Door closers

LCN · Norton · Dorma · Sargent · Yale. Hydraulic adjustment, arm replacement, full closer swap. Speed and latch action set to ANSI A156.4.

Hinges & pivot sets

Continuous hinges, standard butt hinges, pivot sets. Worn pin replacement, full hinge replacement, frame repair after hinge pull-out.

Latches & locksets

Cylindrical, mortise, and exit-device latch bolts. Sticking latch, misaligned strike, broken cylinder or thumb-turn.

Door seals & sweeps

Bottom sweeps, perimeter seals, astragals, intumescent strips. Air infiltration, smoke sealing, and draft control on all door types.

Panic devices & exit hardware

Von Duprin · Detex · Yale · Sargent. Push-bar adjustment, dogging, latch retraction, full device replacement. ANSI A250.8 hardware on every truck.

Fire labels & compliance

Peeling, missing, or painted-over fire labels are a code violation. We replace them with UL-listed labels and document the repair in a written report.

Door frames & closers

Bent, twisted, or pulled-out frames repaired or replaced. We use steel frames with the correct fire rating for the assembly — no wood substitutions in rated openings.

Access control & electric strikes

Electric strikes, magnetic locks, door position switches, and REX sensors. Coordination with your access control system or simple standalone keyed hardware.

What gets checked on every mandoor visit

Our 21-point mandoor inspection covers all mechanical, fire-rating, and operational items. Every visit produces a written report with a deficiency log and photos — suitable for fire marshal and insurance documentation.

Door closer speed (sweep, latch, backcheck) and arm condition
Latch bolt engagement, strike alignment, and bolt throw
Hinge pin wear and hinge plate condition
Bottom sweep seal contact and condition
Perimeter seal and intumescent strip integrity
Fire label condition and legibility
Self-closing function — must close and latch from 5°
Door alignment and frame plumb/square check
Panic device latch retraction and dogging (if present)
Electric strike / magnetic lock function (if present)
Written deficiency report with photo documentation
