Common Garage Door Problems

Is Your Garage Door Giving You Trouble?

Whatever it's doing, there's a reason — and we can fix it today.

01 / 08  ·  Door Won't Open or Close

Call for Same-Day Service
01 / 08

Door Won't Open or Close

Could be a broken spring, dead opener, or sensor misalignment — we diagnose it on-site.

02 / 08

Loud Grinding or Banging

Worn rollers, loose hardware, or a spring under stress. Don't ignore loud noises.

03 / 08

Door Off Its Track

A derailed door is a safety hazard. Call immediately — don't try to force it back.

04 / 08

Broken or Bent Spring

Spring failure is the #1 reason doors stop working. Requires professional service.

05 / 08

Opener Not Responding

Dead batteries, lost programming, or a failed logic board. We handle all brands.

06 / 08

Slow or Uneven Movement

Could be worn cables, a balance issue, or aging rollers. Early service prevents failure.

07 / 08

Door Reverses Before Closing

Safety sensor misalignment or logic board issue. A quick fix that stops a frustrating cycle.

08 / 08

Sagging or Damaged Panel

Cosmetic damage or structural compromise — we assess whether repair or replacement is best.
