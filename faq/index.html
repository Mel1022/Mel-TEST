My door makes a loud noise but still works. Should I call?

Yes. Grinding, scraping, or banging noises are early warning signs of failing components. Addressing them now is always less expensive than an emergency call after a failure.

Springs

My spring broke. Is this dangerous?

A broken spring itself is not dangerous, but attempting to operate the door is. The door will be extremely heavy without spring counterbalance. Call us for same-day service.

Can I replace my own spring?

We strongly advise against it. Garage door springs are under extreme tension — up to 300 lbs of stored force. Improper handling causes serious injuries. Always use a licensed technician.

How long do springs last?

Standard springs are rated for approximately 10,000 cycles (one cycle = door up + door down). For a door used 4 times daily, that's roughly 7 years. High-cycle springs rated for 50,000+ cycles are available.

Openers

What opener brands do you service?

We service all major brands: LiftMaster, Chamberlain, Genie, Craftsman, Marantec, Linear, Overhead Door, Sommer, and more.

My opener remote stopped working. What should I try first?

First, replace the battery. If that doesn't help, try reprogramming the remote (check your manual for instructions). If neither works, call us — it may be a logic board issue or antenna problem.

Can you add a smart phone app to my existing opener?

In many cases, yes. Some older openers can be upgraded with a MyQ or similar smart hub. We'll assess your existing unit and recommend the best path.
