Springs

A broken spring itself is not dangerous, but attempting to operate the door is. The door will be extremely heavy without spring counterbalance. Call us for same-day service.

We strongly advise against it. Garage door springs are under extreme tension — up to 300 lbs of stored force. Improper handling causes serious injuries. Always use a licensed technician.

Standard springs are rated for approximately 10,000 cycles (one cycle = door up + door down). For a door used 4 times daily, that's roughly 7 years. High-cycle springs rated for 50,000+ cycles are available.
